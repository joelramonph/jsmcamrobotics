About Us

Is it worth risking a human life to obtain a scientific fact? At JSM CAM ROBOTICS, we believe that robotics offers a different solution to this fundamental question. Our team has set out to develop a robotic solution to this dilemma.

We have developed EXPLORER SYSTEM

During the WRO 2025 season, the focus was on the theme of "Robots for Life in Space." This system is not simply a robotic solution, but a set of robots working in a synchronized manner. Its main purpose is to detect conditions favorable for life on other planets. The EXPLORER SYSTEM operates through the integration of an exploration robot, a satellite, and a base station.

🎯 Our Objectives

Promote Safety and Reliability

Provide a safe and reliable alternative to crewed space exploration.

Collect Essential Environmental Data

Measure ambient temperature and relative air humidity with the DH11 sensor.

Detect UV Radiation Intensity

Detect the intensity of solar ultraviolet radiation with UV sensors, helping protect humans and equipment.

Identify Liquid Presence

Identify the presence or absence of liquids with liquid level sensors.

Detect Extraterrestrial Vegetation

Use ELECFREAKS AI lens to recognize shapes, colors, and plant patterns, even if their appearance is unusual.

Ensure Habitable Environments

Detect conditions we might face on other planets to ensure the environment is safe and suitable for robot and human activities.

Guarantee Global Connectivity

Maintain connection between the robot and control centers via Simón Bolívar 2 Satellite, relaying messages to the ABAE database.

Centralize Data Analysis and Storage

The ABAE database acts as operations center, handling reception, analysis, and storage of all collected data.

Foster Accessibility and Scalability

Ensure our technology is accessible and has great scalability potential for various applications.

Generate Positive Environmental Impact

Contribute to space exploration with a focus on sustainability and beneficial environmental impact.
